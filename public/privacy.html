隐私与 Cookie 政策

休历默认在本地运行。本政策说明休历保存哪些数据、数据何时会离开你的设备，以及你可以如何管理这些数据。

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 17 日

1. 核心原则

休历无需注册账号，不内置广告、用户画像或访问分析。日历记录、请假信息、工资设置和界面偏好默认保存在你的设备中，项目维护者无法查看这些内容。

2. Cookie 与本地存储

休历不使用广告、分析或跨站追踪 Cookie。浏览器本地存储用于保存语言、主题、视图偏好、应用数据、可选集成配置，以及你已阅读 Cookie 提示的状态。

本地存储不是传统 Cookie，但会保留在浏览器中。你可以通过浏览器的“清除网站数据”功能删除它。

3. 离线缓存

作为可安装的 PWA，休历可能通过 Service Worker 缓存应用文件和公开节假日数据，以便离线使用。该缓存不用于识别或追踪你。

4. 可选外部服务

仅在你主动配置时，休历才会连接外部服务：

  • WebDAV：备份内容和认证信息会发送到你指定的服务，网页版可能通过同源代理转发请求。
  • Google Calendar：所选日历数据会与 Google Calendar API 交换，并受 Google 隐私政策和账号授权控制。
  • 外部链接：GitHub、Telegram 和下载站点由相应平台独立运营。

5. 保留与删除

本地数据会保留到你主动删除、重置应用或清除网站数据。WebDAV 备份和第三方日历数据需要在相应服务中删除；卸载休历不一定会删除远程备份。

6. 安全与联系

请保护设备、浏览器配置和 WebDAV 凭据。建议使用应用专用密码，并仅在可信设备上保存集成配置。隐私问题可通过 GitHub Issues 或 Telegram 联系项目社区。

Privacy by default

Privacy & Cookie Policy

RestCal runs locally by default. This policy explains what RestCal stores, when data may leave your device, and how you can manage it.

Effective: September 17, 2026

1. Core principle

RestCal requires no account and includes no advertising, profiling, or visitor analytics. Calendar records, leave information, salary settings, and interface preferences remain on your device by default. Project maintainers cannot view this content.

2. Cookies and local storage

RestCal does not use advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking cookies. Browser local storage saves language, theme and view preferences, app data, optional integration settings, and your acknowledgement of the cookie notice.

Local storage is not a traditional cookie, but it remains in your browser. Remove it using your browser's “clear site data” controls.

3. Offline cache

As an installable PWA, RestCal may use a Service Worker to cache application files and public holiday data for offline use. This cache is not used to identify or track you.

4. Optional external services

RestCal connects to external services only when you configure them:

  • WebDAV: backup content and authentication details are sent to your chosen provider. The web version may relay requests through a same-origin proxy.
  • Google Calendar: selected calendar data is exchanged with the Google Calendar API under Google's privacy policy and your account permissions.
  • External links: GitHub, Telegram, and download destinations operate independently.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it, reset the app, or clear site data. Delete WebDAV backups and third-party calendar data from the relevant service. Uninstalling RestCal may not remove remote backups.

6. Security and contact

Protect your device, browser profile, and WebDAV credentials. Use an app-specific password and save integration settings only on trusted devices. Ask questions through GitHub Issues or Telegram.

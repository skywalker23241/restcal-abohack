From a day off to a clear record.

Start with your calendar. Leave balances, pay and leave requests follow from it.

Attendance and leave, together

Record attendance, leave, overtime and notes. Switch between day, week, month and year, or mark multiple workdays at once.

Estimate pay before payday

Set salary, fixed deductions and leave deduction rates. Estimate monthly take-home pay and export a payslip image.

Keep track of leave balances

See used and remaining annual or compensatory leave. Review attendance and overtime with monthly charts and yearly heatmaps.

Prepare a leave request

Fill in dates, reason and handoff details. Generate the text, download an image or open your email app with the request.

Plan both legs of your trip

See outbound and return train-ticket sale dates for public holidays. Adjust travel preferences and export reminders to your calendar.

Keep a copy of your records

Records stay on your device by default. Export or import CSV backups, or use WebDAV to back up and restore on another device.

Settings and integrations, built around real work.

Every category below is available in RestCal and saved locally as you make changes.

Profile

Save your name, workplace and hire date for records and leave requests.

Work schedule

Set weekly workdays, daily hours, pay calculation method and default rest days.

Pay & deductions

Configure salary, fixed deductions, tax estimates, payday and leave deduction rates.

Leave allowance

Set annual and compensatory leave allowances, including their expiration rules.

Ticket reminders

Choose outbound and return plans, ticket sale lead time and reminder time.

Leave request template

Reuse the applicant, recipient, common reasons and handoff details.

Accounts & connections

Connect Google Calendar, choose sync content and save a default recipient email.

Data & sync

Import or export CSV, create ICS calendars, and back up or restore with WebDAV.

Appearance

Switch between system, light and dark themes, with Chinese or English.

Advanced settings

Run setup again, export personal settings or reset local data when needed.
